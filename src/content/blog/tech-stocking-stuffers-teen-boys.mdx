## How to Pack a Balanced Stocking for Teens

Aim for the 4-quadrant balance:
* **One useful gadget:** Cable, power bank, or flashlight.
* **One grooming / scent upgrade:** Cologne solid or lip balm.
* **One unique candy/snack:** Imported treats or spicy ramen.
* **One pocket toy/EDC:** Snowflake tool or fidget slider.
